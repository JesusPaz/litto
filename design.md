# Litto — native interface

Same system as AFK: a quiet native study surface, Apple navigation and system typography. The card is the main surface. No decorative dashboard, no custom animation.

## Shared system

Semantic Apple surfaces and text adapt to light/dark and accessibility settings. Blue is the action accent and marks new cards; orange means learning, green means review/done, red means "again" or a destructive action. Every count and state has text ("3 nuevas", "Aprendiendo", "En 8 d"). Colors live in `LittoStyle.swift`.

System UI type only. Spacing: 4/8/12/16/24/32 pt. Native focus/hover/pressed/disabled states and sheets. iPhone targets at least 44 pt. Content stays within a 560 pt readable width.

## Study

One restrained rounded panel (16 pt radius, secondary background) with 16 pt side insets: the front as a large title with pronunciation and a listen button; after reveal, a divider, then image (12 pt radius, at most 240 pt tall), meaning, definition, example with its own listen button and translation, notes, and the system dictionary. The four answers sit below the panel, each showing its title and when the card returns. Keyboard: space reveals, 1–4 answers, S speaks, ⌘Z undoes.

## Mac

Decks in a native sidebar, deck summary and card list in the detail. Study opens as a sheet. Settings show the shared folder Claude writes to.

## iPhone

Native list of decks; study is full screen. Card details are a sheet with photo and file pickers for the image.

## Icon

AFK family: deep ink blue full-bleed, chunky ivory cards on a charcoal base, one warm orange tab. Opaque square generated by `apps/apple/Artwork/make-icon.swift`.
