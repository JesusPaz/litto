# Litto privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

Litto does not collect any data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer never receives your decks, reviews or settings.

- **Your decks and study history** live on your devices. If you are signed in to iCloud, they sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own private iCloud database, which only you can read. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.
- **Images** you add to cards are stored with your decks. When Claude adds an image from a web address, the Mac app downloads it from that address; nothing else is sent.
- **Pronunciation** uses the voices installed on your device and works offline.
- **Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your device.
- **Claude integration** (Mac only): the MCP server inside the app reads and writes your decks on your Mac when you connect it to Claude. What you share with Claude is governed by Anthropic's terms and your Claude settings, not by Litto.

To delete everything, delete your decks in the app (or delete the app) and, if you use iCloud, remove Litto's data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/JesusPaz/litto/issues.
